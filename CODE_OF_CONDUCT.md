# Code of conduct

This project renders the Quran for readers everywhere. Everyone who takes part in it
must be respectful, patient and honest, in issues, reviews, discussions and commits.

- Review the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and what would fix it.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding.
- No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks, no unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Keep discussions on the project. Political and sectarian argument does not belong here.
- Credit others' work and say when you are unsure.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these rules and may block
repeat offenders. To report a problem, email conduct@quran.ws. We handle reports
privately.

This document applies to every space the project runs: the repository, its issues and pull
requests, and any chat or event held in its name.
